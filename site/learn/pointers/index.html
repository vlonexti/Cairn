Pointers and Memory

6 milestones30 lessons30 practice tasks30 projects

A whole course on the thing that makes C and C++ hard: memory. What an address is, reading and writing through pointers, pointer arithmetic and strides, reinterpreting bytes, structs and offsets, following chains of pointers, the stack, the heap and object lifetime, ownership, leaks, smart pointers and views, and finally writing your own arena, pool and free-list allocators and seeing how virtual memory and pages work. It assumes the first milestones of the C++ course.
