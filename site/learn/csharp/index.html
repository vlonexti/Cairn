C#

8 milestones41 lessons52 practice tasks40 projects

Modern C on .NET, from the first line to a complete, tested application. The course covers types, strings and control flow, methods, collections and LINQ, pattern matching, classes, records, interfaces and inheritance, generics, delegates and events, exceptions and resources, async and await, files and JSON, and how real applications are structured: projects and NuGet, unit tests, dependency injection and desktop UI, and finally the advanced language: operators and indexers, collections in depth, reflection and attributes, Span and performance, regular expressions, and async streams with channels, then LINQ in depth, every kind of pattern, generic math, dates and culture, and variance. Practice compiles and runs on .NET 9 from your browser.
